Our Mission: Charting the Future of Autonomous Navigation

<p class="about-eyebrow">BEYOND THE JOYSTICK</p>

<p class="about-hero__intro">
  Welcome to Beyond The Joystick, your professional resource dedicated
  to the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and autonomous rover
  navigation.
</p>

<div class="about-hero__text">

  <p>
    The 20-minute communication lag to Mars isn't just a technical
    challenge; it's the fundamental reason this field exists.
    We can't simply "joystick" rovers on other worlds. They must think,
    perceive, and act on their own.
  </p>

  <p>
    Our mission is to explore and explain the complex technologies,
    from SLAM algorithms to perception sensors, that allow robotic
    explorers to navigate independently. We bridge the gap between
    complex academic theory and real-world application, covering
    missions from Mars to the Moon and projects you can build on your
    own workbench.
  </p>

</div>

<div class="about-founder__story">

  <p>
    Ever since I first discovered how machines could sense and interact
    with their environment, I’ve been fascinated by the challenge of
    building intelligent systems capable of exploring — and understanding
    — the unknown. That curiosity naturally evolved into a passion for
    <strong>aerospace engineering and autonomous robotics</strong>,
    fields where technology and exploration meet.
  </p>

  <p>
    I’m currently pursuing a
    <strong>degree in Aerospace Engineering at Politecnico di Milano</strong>,
    where I also serve as an <strong>engineer and team leader</strong>
    in <strong>PoliSpace</strong>, the university’s student space association.
    Within <strong>RoverTech</strong>, I design and program the
    <strong>locomotion and sensing systems</strong> of our Mars rover
    prototype, working primarily with
    <strong>embedded systems (STM32, C, Rust)</strong> and sensor integration.
    In parallel, I lead the <strong>IT Team</strong>, developing internal
    tools and managing project workflows — experiences that have honed
    both my <strong>technical and leadership</strong> skills.
  </p>

  <p>
    I created this platform to share
    <strong>practical insights, technical guides, and reflections</strong>
    at the intersection of
    <strong>robotics, autonomy, and space exploration</strong>.
    My goal is to make advanced topics approachable while maintaining
    the depth and rigor they deserve — building a community for those who,
    like me, believe that
    <strong>autonomy is the next frontier of exploration</strong>.
  </p>

</div>
