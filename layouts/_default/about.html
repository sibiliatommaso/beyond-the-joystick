BEYOND THE JOYSTICK

{{ .Title }}

Welcome to Beyond The Joystick, your professional resource dedicated to the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and autonomous rover navigation.

The 20-minute communication lag to Mars isn't just a technical challenge; it's the fundamental reason this field exists. We can't simply "joystick" rovers on other worlds. They must think, perceive, and act on their own.

Our mission is to explore and explain the complex technologies, from SLAM algorithms to perception sensors, that allow robotic explorers to navigate independently. We bridge the gap between complex academic theory and real-world application, covering missions from Mars to the Moon and projects you can build on your own workbench.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECT

Meet The Founder

Hi, I'm Tommaso Sibilia.

Founder and chief editor of Beyond The Joystick.

You can find my professional profile on LinkedIn.

Ever since I first discovered how machines could sense and interact with their environment, I’ve been fascinated by the challenge of building intelligent systems capable of exploring — and understanding — the unknown. That curiosity naturally evolved into a passion for aerospace engineering and autonomous robotics, fields where technology and exploration meet.

I’m currently pursuing a degree in Aerospace Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, where I also serve as an engineer and team leader in PoliSpace, the university’s student space association. Within RoverTech, I design and program the locomotion and sensing systems of our Mars rover prototype, working primarily with embedded systems (STM32, C, Rust) and sensor integration. In parallel, I lead the IT Team, developing internal tools and managing project workflows — experiences that have honed both my technical and leadership skills.

I created this platform to share practical insights, technical guides, and reflections at the intersection of robotics, autonomy, and space exploration. My goal is to make advanced topics approachable while maintaining the depth and rigor they deserve — building a community for those who, like me, believe that autonomy is the next frontier of exploration.
